# Pratt & Whitney (pratt-and-whitney)

A major American aerospace manufacturer designing, manufacturing, and servicing aircraft engines for commercial, military, and business aviation applications. A division of RTX Corporation, Pratt & Whitney is known for its geared turbofan (GTF) engine technology, the F135 powering the F-35, and the PT6 turboprop family. Customer-facing digital services - EngineWise health monitoring, FAST data services, and the Customer Portal - are operated privately for airline, MRO, and military customers behind credentialed access and are not exposed as a public developer API.
